# Product Documentation & Development Guidelines

# 1. Product Overview

## Product Name

Drowsie

---

## Product Category

Mobile Utility / Wellness / Productivity

---

# Product Vision

Create a modern alarm application that transforms waking up from a stressful interruption into a calming daily ritual.

Unlike traditional alarm applications that focus primarily on forcing users awake through pressure or aggressive interactions, this application prioritizes:

* emotional comfort
* sustainable wake habits
* aesthetic user experience
* mindful morning routines
* adaptive wake systems

---

# Core Philosophy

## Traditional Alarm Apps

> “Wake users up at any cost.”

## Our Product

> “Help users wake up consistently without hating mornings.”

---

# Primary Goals

The app should:

* reduce wake-up anxiety
* increase wake consistency
* create emotional attachment to mornings
* provide a premium and calming user experience
* feel cozy and intentional rather than clinical

---

# Target Audience

## Primary Audience

* Gen Z users
* students
* young professionals
* productivity enthusiasts
* aesthetic app users
* wellness-focused users

---

## Secondary Audience

* users with irregular sleep schedules
* users who dislike aggressive alarm apps
* people interested in mindfulness
* users seeking healthier routines

---

# Brand Personality

The app should feel:

* calm
* warm
* soft
* premium
* intentional
* emotionally intelligent

Avoid:

* harshness
* aggressive UX
* clutter
* mechanical interactions
* excessive gamification

---

# 2. Product Positioning

# Positioning Statement

A cozy alarm app designed to make mornings calmer, more intentional, and emotionally sustainable.

---

# Key Differentiators

| Traditional Alarm Apps | This App              |

| Loud and stressful     | Calm and ambient      |
| Punishment-based       | Guidance-based        |
| Utility-focused        | Experience-focused    |

# Emotional Positioning

The app should feel like:

* a calm bedside companion
* a peaceful morning assistant
* a warm daily ritual

NOT:

* a productivity punishment tool
* a strict alarm system
* a stressful utility app

---

# 3. Design System Guidelines

# Design Language

## Visual Tone

* soft
* ambient
* minimal
* tactile
* emotionally warm

---

# Color Palette Guidelines

Prefer:

* warm neutrals
* muted gradients
* low saturation
* soft contrast

Avoid:

* overly bright colors
* neon tones
* harsh blacks
* high contrast warning aesthetics

---

# Typography Guidelines

Use:

* clean rounded fonts
* highly readable type
* generous spacing

Typography should feel:

* breathable
* elegant
* calming

---

# Animation Guidelines

Animations should:

* feel smooth and slow
* reduce cognitive load
* reinforce calmness

Avoid:

* aggressive motion
* excessive bouncing
* rapid transitions
* overstimulation

---

# UI Philosophy

Every screen should answer:

> “Does this reduce stress or increase it?”

---

# Haptic Guidelines

Haptics should:

* feel subtle
* feel intentional
* reinforce comfort

Avoid:

* sharp feedback
* excessive vibration patterns

---

# Sound Design Guidelines

The audio system is critical.

Use:

* ambient sounds
* nature-inspired tones
* soft wake transitions
* layered audio progression

Avoid:

* jarring sounds
* default digital alarms
* repetitive high-pitch tones

---

# 5. User Experience Principles

# Core UX Principles

## Principle 1 — Reduce Stress

Every interaction should minimize friction and anxiety.

---

## Principle 2 — Intentional Simplicity

Do not overload screens with options.

---

## Principle 3 — Emotional Design

Users should emotionally enjoy opening the app.

---

## Principle 4 — Calm First

Comfort takes priority over hyper-productivity aesthetics.

---

## Principle 5 — Minimal Cognitive Load

The app should feel effortless during sleepy states.

---

# UX Anti-Patterns To Avoid

Avoid:

* cluttered dashboards
* aggressive red warning colors
* too many notifications
* excessive analytics
* loud onboarding
* overwhelming settings pages
